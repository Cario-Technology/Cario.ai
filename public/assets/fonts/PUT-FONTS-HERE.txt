NEUZEIT GROTESK - font files go in this folder
==============================================
Copy your purchased files here, renamed exactly:
  NeuzeitGrotesk-Light.woff2      (or .woff / .otf / .ttf)
  NeuzeitGrotesk-Regular.woff2
  NeuzeitGrotesk-Bold.woff2
  NeuzeitGrotesk-Black.woff2
Only Regular and Bold are essential.
